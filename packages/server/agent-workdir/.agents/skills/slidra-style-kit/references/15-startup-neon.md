# 15 · startup-neon

**First-impression feel**: Near-black base with magenta-to-purple gradient glow. Like a product launch at 7 PM — bright, fast, a bit loud, but you'll remember it.

**Suited for**: Product launches, pitch decks, recruiting, any context that needs a memorable moment.
**Not suited for**: Internal reviews, risk explanations. This style is inherently optimistic and ill-suited for bad news.

**Why this palette**: Magenta and purple are adjacent hues, so together they "glow" rather than "clash"; the base retains a hint of blue so the glow has room to spread; text is pure white — this brightness demands the strongest contrast.
